# Banablur — Privacy policy

**Last updated:** 21 September 2026

Banablur is a browser extension. It runs locally in your browser.

## What Banablur does

Banablur removes age-verification overlays and CSS / player blur on sites you open yourself. An optional download button can save a video the page already exposes. All network requests use **your own IP**. There is no proxy and no traffic rerouting.

## Data we collect

**None.** Banablur does not send your browsing history, videos, account data, or identifiers to the developer or to any analytics service.

## Data stored on your device

Settings such as “auto cleanup” and learned site profiles are saved with the browser `storage` API on **your** computer or profile. They stay local unless you sync your browser profile with Google/Mozilla yourself.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|------------|-----|
| `storage` | Save your toggles and learned domains locally |
| `activeTab` | The popup talks to the tab you have open |
| `downloads` | Optional video download through the browser download manager |
| Host access (XVIDEOS / XNXX TLDs) | Fetch the site’s own embed/stream metadata from your IP |
| Content scripts | Detect overlays and blur on the page you are viewing |

## What we do not do

- No accounts, no sign-in to Banablur
- No telemetry, ads network, or crash reporting
- No sale of data
- No remote code fetched from our servers
